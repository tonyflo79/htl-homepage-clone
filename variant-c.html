The Cyber Threat to Your Home Equity

The threat of title fraud is real—and the impact can be devastating.

Cyber-enabled title fraud exploits digitized public record systems to steal homeownership. Criminals forge documents electronically, file them with county recorders who perform no identity verification, and borrow against your home or sell it — all without your knowledge. 

How does cyber-enabled title fraud work?

Public property records are fully digitized. County recording systems accept electronic filings without identity verification — everything a cyber criminal needs is accessible online.

What happens when a forged deed is filed?

Digitally forged documents are filed with your county recorder, transferring your property ownership to a stranger — without a single identity check.

What can a cyber criminal do with your title?

The cyber criminal leverages your property records to take out loans against your equity or sells the home — exploiting the same digital systems that failed to verify the original filing.

Why is resolution so costly without cyber defense?

The criminal and the cash are gone. Without early cyber detection, you face $50,000–$150,000 in legal costs and two to five years proving you are the victim — a nightmare that early monitoring collapses to weeks.
